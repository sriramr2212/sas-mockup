A Family Story

From our father's loom to your wardrobe.

Sri Aishwarya Sarees began in 1972 when our father set out across Tamil Nadu, Andhra and Karnataka, sitting with weavers and choosing yarn by hand. Three generations later, the same families still weave for us — from our boutiques in T.Nagar and Adyar to homes across the world, every saree carries a quiet promise of patience, of authenticity, and of the hands that made it.
